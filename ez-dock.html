EZ Dock Solutions

Complete waterfront dock systems designed for Nova Scotia conditions

Professional Dock Solutions for Your Waterfront

As authorized EZ Dock dealers and installers, we provide complete waterfront solutions from consultation through installation and ongoing maintenance. Our dock systems are engineered for durability, stability, and ease of use in Atlantic Canada's challenging marine environment.

Our EZ Dock Services

EZ Dock Sales & Consultation

Expert guidance on selecting the right dock configuration for your waterfront. We assess site conditions, water depth, usage requirements, and aesthetic preferences to design a custom solution. Choose from a full range of EZ Dock products and accessories.

Professional Installation

Certified installers ensure your dock system is properly configured and anchored. We handle all permitting requirements, site preparation, and installation logistics. Our team works efficiently to minimize disruption while delivering flawless results.

Seasonal Removal & Storage

Protect your investment with professional seasonal services. We safely remove your dock before winter, provide secure storage, and reinstall when you're ready. Optional cleaning and maintenance performed during storage period.

Maintenance & Repair

Keep your dock system in optimal condition with regular maintenance. We provide cleaning, hardware inspection, section replacement, and accessory upgrades. Emergency repair services available for unexpected issues.

Custom Configuration Design

Expand your waterfront capabilities with custom dock configurations. Add boat lifts, kayak launches, swim platforms, gangways, and more. We design systems that grow with your needs and integrate seamlessly with existing structures.
