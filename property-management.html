Professional Property Management for Peace of Mind

At Nova Solutions, we understand that property ownership should be rewarding, not stressful. Our comprehensive property management services handle every aspect of your investment, allowing you to enjoy the benefits without the day-to-day hassles.

What We Offer

Comprehensive Tenant Screening

We implement a rigorous multi-step screening process that includes credit checks, employment verification, rental history review, and reference validation. Our thorough approach ensures you get reliable, responsible tenants who will treat your property with respect.

Professional Leasing Services

From marketing your property to negotiating lease terms, we handle the entire leasing process. We ensure competitive pricing, legal compliance, and professional documentation that protects your interests.

24/7 Maintenance Coordination

Our network of licensed, insured contractors ensures prompt response to maintenance requests. We handle everything from routine repairs to emergency situations, with 24/7 availability for urgent issues.

Regular Property Inspections

Quarterly inspections document property condition, identify maintenance needs early, and ensure lease compliance. Our detailed reports include photos and recommendations for preventive maintenance.

Transparent Financial Reporting

Monthly statements provide complete transparency on income, expenses, and property performance. Our online portal gives you 24/7 access to financial data, maintenance records, and property documents.

Legal Compliance & Risk Management

Stay compliant with evolving landlord-tenant laws. We handle lease enforcement, eviction proceedings when necessary, and ensure all documentation meets legal requirements to protect you from liability.
