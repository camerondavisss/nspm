Property Management

What does your property management service include?

Our comprehensive property management includes:

  • Tenant screening and placement
  • Rent collection and financial reporting
  • 24/7 maintenance coordination
  • Quarterly property inspections
  • Legal compliance and documentation
  • Lease enforcement and renewals
  • Online owner portal access
How do you screen potential tenants?

We employ a rigorous multi-step screening process:

  • Credit history check
  • Employment verification
  • Previous landlord references
  • Criminal background check
  • Income verification (typically 3x rent requirement)
  • Rental history review

This thorough approach helps ensure reliable, responsible tenants for your property.

What are your management fees?

Our fee structure is transparent and competitive. Management fees vary based on property type, number of units, and services required. Contact us for a personalized quote that outlines all costs with no hidden fees. We typically charge a percentage of monthly rent plus any applicable leasing fees.

How do I access my property financial reports?

Property owners have 24/7 access to our online portal where you can view:

  • Monthly financial statements
  • Income and expense reports
  • Maintenance requests and history
  • Inspection reports with photos
  • Lease documents and renewals

We also provide monthly email summaries and are always available for questions.

Commercial Lawn Care

How often will you service my property?

We offer flexible service schedules based on your needs:

  • Weekly service during growing season (most popular)
  • Bi-weekly maintenance programs
  • Custom schedules for specific requirements

We assign the same crew to your property for consistency and familiarity with your site's specific needs.

What happens if you can't service on the scheduled day due to weather?

Weather is unpredictable, especially in Nova Scotia. If we can't complete your service on the scheduled day, we'll reschedule within the same week whenever possible. You'll be notified of any changes, and we'll ensure your property receives all contracted services.

Do you provide services year-round?

Yes! We offer seasonal services including:

  • Spring: Cleanup, fertilization, bed preparation
  • Summer: Regular mowing, edging, weed control
  • Fall: Leaf removal, aeration, winterization
  • Winter: Snow removal and ice management (upon request)

Luxury Landscaping

How does the design process work?

Our design process includes:

  • Initial Consultation: We visit your property to discuss your vision, needs, and budget
  • Site Analysis: Assessment of soil, drainage, sun exposure, and existing conditions
  • Design Development: Creation of detailed plans with material selections
  • Presentation: Review of design with 3D renderings and cost estimate
  • Refinement: Adjustments based on your feedback
  • Installation: Professional execution of the approved plan
How long does a typical landscape project take?

Project timelines vary based on scope and complexity:

  • Small projects (patio, garden bed): 1-3 days
  • Medium projects (large patio, retaining walls): 1-2 weeks
  • Large projects (complete landscape renovation): 2-6 weeks

We provide detailed timelines during the proposal phase and keep you informed throughout the project.

Do you offer warranties on your work?

Yes! We stand behind our work with comprehensive warranties:

  • 1-year warranty on plant materials
  • 2-year warranty on hardscape installation
  • 5-year warranty on retaining wall structures

Warranty details are included in every project proposal.

EZ Dock Solutions

What makes EZ Dock different from traditional docks?

EZ Dock offers several advantages:

  • Maintenance-Free: No painting, staining, or sealing required
  • Durable: Polyethylene construction resists weather, UV, and marine growth
  • Stable: Superior stability compared to floating docks
  • Modular: Easy to expand or reconfigure
  • Safe: Non-slip surface, no splinters or exposed hardware
  • Eco-Friendly: No chemicals leaching into water
Do I need permits for dock installation?

Most dock installations in Nova Scotia require permits from:

  • Nova Scotia Department of Fisheries and Aquaculture
  • Transport Canada (for navigable waters)
  • Local municipal authorities

We handle all permitting requirements as part of our service, ensuring compliance with all regulations.

When should I have my dock removed for winter?

We typically recommend dock removal in late October or early November, before ice formation. The exact timing depends on:

  • Your specific location
  • Historical freeze dates for your area
  • Current weather forecasts
  • Ice movement patterns in your waterway

We'll contact you in advance to schedule removal at the optimal time. Spring reinstallation typically occurs in April or May.
